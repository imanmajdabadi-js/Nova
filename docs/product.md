# Product

## Overview

NOVA is a Persian-first platform for discovering and booking urban experiences, creative spaces, workshops and events. It connects people who offer local experiences with people who want to take part in them.

The platform supports three roles: Customer, Host, and Admin. The primary language is Persian (`fa`), with English (`en`) as a secondary locale. The primary writing direction is RTL.

## Who Uses It

- **Customer** — searches and books experiences, manages personal bookings and preferences.
- **Host** — publishes and manages experiences, tracks bookings and customers, views analytics.
- **Admin** — manages platform-wide users, hosts, experiences, categories, and reports.

## Main User Journeys

### Customer Journey

1. Browse the home page and discover featured experiences.
2. Explore the catalog by category, location, date, or search.
3. View an experience detail page to read the description, schedule, price, location, and reviews.
4. Book an experience, providing guest details and choosing options/schedule.
5. Receive a booking confirmation.
6. View upcoming, past, and canceled bookings in the dashboard.
7. Manage a wishlist of saved experiences.
8. Manage personal profile and notification/payment settings.

### Host Journey

1. Register and set up a host profile.
2. Create a new experience, including title, description, media, schedule, availability, pricing, and location.
3. Manage existing experiences (edit, archive, republish).
4. View incoming bookings and their statuses.
5. Manage customer lists and answer questions.
6. Review analytics on bookings, revenue, and engagement.
7. Update host and payment settings.

### Admin Journey

1. Access the admin panel.
2. Browse and search platform users and hosts.
3. Review reported experiences and content.
4. Manage experience categories and taxonomy.
5. Review bookings across the platform.
6. Review reported content and issues.
7. Adjust platform-wide settings.
